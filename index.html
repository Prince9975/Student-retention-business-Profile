Student Retention Tracking & Reporting System

A technology-based solution designed to help higher education institutions identify non-returning students, manage follow-up communication, understand student concerns, and support student retention.

Learn More

About the System

The Student Retention Tracking and Reporting System is a desktop-based research prototype designed for higher education institutions.


The system identifies students who have not completed registration after an academic registration deadline and creates retention cases that Student Affairs staff can monitor and manage.


It supports communication through channels such as Email, WhatsApp and SMS, while conversational artificial intelligence assists with processing student responses and identifying cases that may require human intervention.

System Features

Student Monitoring

Tracks student registration information and identifies eligible non-returning students after registration deadlines.

Retention Cases

Automatically creates and manages retention cases for students requiring follow-up.

Multi-Channel Communication

Supports student follow-up through Email, WhatsApp and SMS.

AI-Assisted Responses

Uses conversational AI to help understand student responses and support appropriate follow-up.

Staff Escalation

Cases requiring human support can be escalated to Student Affairs staff for further attention.

Reports & Monitoring

Provides dashboards, communication history and reports to support retention monitoring and decision-making.

How It Works

The system combines registration information, automated communication and staff intervention to support the student retention process.

Registration Deadline → Non-Returning Student Detection → Retention Case → Student Follow-Up → Student Response → AI Processing → Staff Support → Resolution
